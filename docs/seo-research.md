Crawler guidance: Google's AI Features documentation says normal search eligibility applies and no special AI files or schema are required: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features. Google's structured data documentation requires visible, accurate markup: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data. Bing's guidance covers indexing and Copilot experiences: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a. OpenAI's bots documentation distinguishes OAI-SearchBot from other crawlers: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots. The site uses `User-agent: * / Allow: /`, a sitemap, concise visible prose and only matching Person, WebSite, ProfilePage, CreativeWork and BreadcrumbList markup. No llms.txt is added.

Indexable launch pages: /, /about/, /work/, /work/sl6dl7/, /expertise/creative-direction/, /expertise/social-media-strategy/, /project-monet/, /contact/.
